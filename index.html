About Technovation Club

Empowering Students Through Innovation & Technology

Who We Are?

Technovation Club is the official Networking Club of ABES Engineering College. We bring together students passionate about networking, cybersecurity, emerging technologies, innovation, and real-world problem solving.

Our aim is to build a collaborative community where students learn, innovate, connect, develop practical skills, and create impactful technology-driven solutions for the future.

Our Mission

To inspire students to innovate and solve real-world problems using technology.

Our Vision

To become one of the best student technical communities by encouraging creativity and teamwork.
